THE TRUST LAYER

Know where every number comes from.

Solar production is supplied by an independent scientific model. Financial results are a transparent simulation of inputs you can change—not a quote or a guaranteed return.

01 /

Solar resource

European Commission JRC PVGIS 5.3, using historical satellite and reanalysis weather data. Actual roof shading and equipment performance may differ.

Official documentation ↗
02 /

Electricity benchmarks

Eurostat's household electricity survey is context, not a substitute for your avoidable per-kWh tariff. Price, credits and installation costs stay user-editable.

Explore Eurostat data ↗
03 /

Greek settlement rules

The model values self-used and exported energy separately; it does not promise net metering, fixed export rates or unconfirmed subsidies.

Greek ministry guidance ↗

Method limits: Monthly energy balance assumes flat monthly demand and an editable direct-consumption percentage. It does not perform hourly load matching, model batteries, debt, taxation, fixed utility fees, dynamic export pricing, roof obstructions or grid constraints. Obtain a site survey and written offers before investing.
