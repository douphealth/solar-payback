03 / TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

A good forecast should show its working.

Solar physics and household economics belong to different datasets. We make that distinction explicit rather than hiding estimates behind a single magic number.

01 / SOLAR DATA

Location, not guesswork.

The European Commission's PVGIS calculates a location- and orientation-specific typical year using historical irradiance and weather records.

02 / YOUR INPUTS

Your bills, your assumptions.

You control electricity prices, export credit, household demand, self-consumption and installation cost. These are never presented as verified live supplier quotes.

03 / LIFETIME MODEL

Year-by-year accountability.

The forecast includes generation degradation, annual expenses, inverter replacement, inflation assumptions, discounting and export revenue. Export the full schedule.
