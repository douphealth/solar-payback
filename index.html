PRIVACY & DATA RETENTION

Local-First Processing

Imported 15/30/60-minute electricity meter CSVs and quote figures are parsed in-memory in your browser. Scenarios are stored in your browser's localStorage and can be exported or cleared at any time.

TERMS & ENGINEERING SCOPE

Decision Aid, Not a Site Survey

SolarPayback Aurora provides independent financial and physical modeling. It does not replace a licensed structural engineer's roof load inspection, on-site shade measurement, licensed electrician sign-off, or tax advisor review.

PRICING, ENTITLEMENT & REFUNDS

Free Synopsis vs. Paid Audit Report

The free tier provides an unverified location PVGIS estimate and preliminary synopsis. Paid server-signed reports (€29 single-quote / €59 3-quote comparison) require verified Stripe Checkout, store immutable PDFs in private R2 storage, and include a 14-day refund & GDPR erasure right (DELETE /api/customer/data).

Methodology boundary

PVGIS and NREL benchmarks describe historical solar resource; they do not inspect your exact roof obstacles, predict future retail electricity prices, verify your utility bill, guarantee a subsidy or replace licensed electrical engineering or tax advice.
